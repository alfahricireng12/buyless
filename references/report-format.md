# Shopping report

Default to the destination country/region's language, unless the user explicitly requests another language. Apply the multilingual selection rules in [international.md](international.md). Localize headings and explanations consistently while preserving product names and currency codes. Keep the first answer useful without opening an attachment.

Lead with one best qualifying, comparable offer among those checked, including total, seller, location/fulfillment, and a direct product source. If no complete total is supported, lead with the best partial comparison and say why a cheapest payable total cannot yet be named. Do not silently recommend a higher-priced store merely because it is familiar.

Answer the purchase decision first: where to buy the requested product for the lowest supported comparable cost, why the seller qualifies, and what remains unknown. Reliability checks apply even without an explicit authenticity request. Do not substitute generic shopping advice, search queries or a list of storefront homepages for the comparison the host can perform. If a final step requires the user's account, address or seller contact, state that specific remaining step after completing the independent research.

The first response should let a non-expert act without reading a long report. Use a compact lead such as:

> **Best buy among checked offers:** exact product and seller with a direct product link
>
> **Total payable:** item − confirmed immediate coupon + known shipping and fees; label an incomplete estimate.
>
> **Why this seller qualifies:** concrete seller, warranty/return, buyer-protection and product-match observations.
>
> **Main risk:** the most consequential unknown or conflict.
>
> **Checked:** time and timezone.

Then show at most three useful alternatives. Include a cheaper but unverified listing if it could change the user's decision, clearly separated from recommended offers. Avoid twenty-result dumps. Give detailed source coverage or arithmetic when requested or when necessary to explain a close call.

State the working assumptions briefly: one new item, delivery to the supplied city, destination currency, and destination language, unless the request overrides them. Shipping is included only where observed; otherwise label it unknown. Budget, condition and pickup radius need not be asked up front. For pickup, compare the store payment separately from delivered cost because travel cost is unknown.

Use localized plain-language price labels where supported: **Total verified**, **Coupon found**, **Shipping unknown**, **Warranty unclear**, **Seller risk**, **Used item**, and **Variant mismatch**. “Total verified” means the observed listing, promotion and known charges support the amount for the stated destination and conditions; it does not mean a completed personal checkout. Keep price status distinct from evidence about seller and product.

For trust, use localized statuses equivalent to **Strong evidence**, **Sufficient evidence**, **Needs more checking**, and **Avoid for now**. Explain the underlying observations in one sentence. Strong evidence requires independent, relevant support for exact product identity, seller/channel and applicable transaction protections with no material unresolved conflict. Sufficient evidence supports a recommendation under the ordinary gate but may lack manufacturer authorization or unit-level inspection. A missing source alone does not justify “Avoid for now”; reserve it for unresolved material conflicts or concrete transaction red flags. No status guarantees an individual physical unit is genuine. Never use a fabricated confidence percentage or a generic trust score.

When comparison needs a table, keep it small:

| Product / seller | Payment and fees | Coupon conditions | Pickup / delivery | Evidence and source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Group different products, fulfillment modes, currencies, and incomplete quotes separately. Include a small number of useful alternatives; do not pad the answer with low-quality results.

For the leading offer show:

- Item amount minus immediate coupon plus shipping and known fees.
- Unknown charges, cashback separately, and any conditional eligibility.
- Exact model, variant, condition, quantity, and relevant warranty.
- Source links and observation date/time.
- What supports the seller/product claims and what remains unverified.

End with a short coverage statement: country and locality/radius reference, stores/pages actually checked, source families attempted, blocked sources, stop reason and high-impact unfinished checks. Keep internal ledger detail out of the lead unless it changes the decision. Store discovery is dynamic; do not describe coverage as a fixed supported-store list.

For authenticity-constrained requests, identify the cheapest evidence-qualified offer and show cheaper unverified listings separately. State the evidence level and unresolved conflicts, not a guarantee or invented confidence percentage. If no candidate qualifies, say so. A complete evidence record does not make an incomplete delivery price a confirmed cheapest total.

Use “lowest among the comparable offers checked,” not “cheapest everywhere.” Do not call the output a live search if it comes from supplied data or examples.

If the user requests a saved report, exclude precise personal locations and account-specific details from a shareable version. The optional calculator emits Markdown/JSON; its result is arithmetic support, not a replacement for the evidence summary.
