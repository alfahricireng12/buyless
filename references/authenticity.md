# Authenticity: evidence before reassurance

The objective is the cheapest matching offer that satisfies the user's constraints. When genuine/original goods are required, compare prices within the evidence-qualified set and show cheaper unverified listings separately. If none qualify, say so. Do not silently relax the requirement or guarantee physical authenticity.

## Investigate four separate questions

1. **Product identity:** match the exact manufacturer model/part number, specifications, variant, regional code and condition. Compare against manufacturer documentation. A matching title or photograph cannot establish what will be delivered.
2. **Seller and supply channel:** trace an authorization claim to the manufacturer's own current country-specific directory or a statement identifying the exact seller entity and relevant product line. Independently navigate to the real brand domain. A store name containing "Official", a marketplace badge, a company registration or a business address is not equivalent to brand authorization. Directory absence is unverified status, not proof of counterfeit goods.
3. **Warranty and transaction protection:** verify the seller's warranty terms, territory, issuer, return window, exclusions, payment protection and whether an overseas purchase qualifies. Manufacturer warranty and seller-only warranty are different. A strong seller history helps assess transactions but does not authenticate an individual unit.
4. **Individual unit:** look for independent, unit-specific inspection evidence where available. Record who inspected it, when, identifiers, scope and limitations. Serial/warranty lookups can support consistency checks; do not treat a valid serial alone as proof that a listing or delivered unit is genuine. Never publish private identifiers in a shared report.

For used/refurbished goods, also check replaced parts, activation/ownership locks, battery/condition descriptions and what "refurbished" means for that seller. Factory specifications do not prove that a used device still has its original parts. Category-specific evidence matters: provenance/inspection for collectibles, authorized distribution and batch/expiry for consumables, region/parts/warranty for electronics. Avoid inventing a universal authentication method.

## Evidence states and recommendation gate

Maintain separate claims with source URL, inspected time, exact subject, authority, supporting observation, contradiction and unresolved gap. Trace reposted statements to their origin rather than counting copies as corroboration.

- **Documented authorized channel:** exact model supported by manufacturer information, seller authorization established for this entity/category/territory, applicable warranty and returns documented, no unresolved material contradictions. This supports a purchase recommendation, not a physical authenticity guarantee.
- **Documented unit inspection:** credible inspection explicitly covers the offered individual unit; state precisely what was inspected. Upcoming platform inspection or an eligible badge is a future safeguard, not completed inspection. Evaluate transaction terms separately.
- **Insufficient evidence:** seller claims, ratings, snippets, generic certificates, missing authorization or unavailable pages. Explain the gap without calling the seller fraudulent.
- **Unresolved conflict:** contradictory model, entity, provenance, inspection or warranty evidence. Keep outside the evidence-qualified set until resolved; present both sources.

Do not manufacture percentage confidence or a composite trust score. The optional offline audit only checks the documented-authorized-channel path and the consistency of supplied records. Other evidence paths require explicit host reasoning; do not force them into a misleading machine label.

## Deeper investigation that changes a decision

For leading candidates, search exact seller identity + model + relevant complaint/return terms in the local language. Distinguish recent, specific, independently attributable patterns from copied accusations or unrelated names. Look for domain/entity mismatches and suspicious off-platform payment requirements. Missing complaints is not proof of safety. Never let a negative review alone become a definitive scam accusation.

If verification needs a seller response, provide the precise unanswered question; do not send a message without user authorization. Recheck the winning listing and volatile claims where possible. At delivery, manufacturer or qualified professional verification may still be needed; mention this only when relevant to an unresolved authenticity question.

## Examples of narrowly scoped official resources

- [Lenovo warranty lookup](https://pcsupport.lenovo.com/lc/en/warranty-lookup) supports warranty research; it is not sufficient by itself to establish a listing's authenticity.
- [eBay Authenticity Guarantee](https://www.ebay.com/help/help/buying/buying-authenticity-guarantee/buying-authenticity-guarantee?id=5470) describes inspection for eligible transactions. Read the current category, listing and destination conditions. Do not assume every item, seller or country participates.

Always read current official guidance for the actual product and destination. These examples are not integrations, endorsements or blanket verification services.
