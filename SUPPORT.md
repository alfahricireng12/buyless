# Support

BuyLess is maintained by devino.als.

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/alfahricireng12/buyless/issues) for bug reports, incorrect behavior, regional-source suggestions, and privacy concerns. Include the AI host, destination city and country, product request, observed result, and any non-sensitive reproduction details.

Do not post addresses, account credentials, payment information, private order details, or other sensitive personal data.
