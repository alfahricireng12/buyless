## Short description

Find trusted deals nearby.

## Long description

Tell BuyLess what you want and where you are. It guides your AI through nearby stores, domestic retailers, marketplaces, and destination-eligible overseas sellers; compares known payable costs; checks coupon conditions; and explains seller, warranty, and authenticity evidence. It presents one recommended offer first and keeps cheaper but insufficiently verified alternatives separate.

## Starter prompts

1. I want to buy a laptop in Jakarta.
2. Find the cheapest genuine headphones in Miami.
3. Compare local and overseas camera prices in Osaka.

## Availability

Worldwide where the OpenAI Plugins Directory and public web research are supported, subject to local law and host availability.

## Initial release notes

Initial public submission of BuyLess 0.5.0, a skills-only shopping research plugin. It requires a product and location, discovers stores dynamically rather than using a fixed store list, compares known total costs and coupon conditions, applies evidence-based seller checks, and clearly reports unresolved price or authenticity limitations. It performs no purchases or seller contact.
