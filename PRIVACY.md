# Privacy

BuyLess is a skills-only plugin maintained by devino.als. It does not operate a BuyLess account, backend, analytics service, or shared API key. The package itself does not collect, transmit, sell, or retain personal data.

BuyLess instructs the user's AI host to research public shopping information. Search terms can include a product and a city or country supplied by the user. The host and websites it accesses process that activity under their own privacy terms. Users should provide a city or broad region rather than a precise home address.

BuyLess does not purchase products, contact sellers, create accounts, or submit personal or payment information. Those actions require a separate explicit user request and are outside the default skill workflow.

Report privacy concerns through the public support channel listed in `SUPPORT.md`.
